# Revision explanation

The latest editorial closeout strengthens the empirical contribution, defines the leather target as retrieval of the assigned registered-swatch ID, and separates current controlled comparisons from unreconstructed historical ablations. Figure-to-supplement cross-references were corrected in the local manuscript. Its text, tables and scientific conclusions retain their evidence boundaries.

Version 2.1.0 adds the fixed-727 reference-investment companion to the public code/numerical release. Center, A5, A30, RGB fusion and two original references use the same 727 records, 725 distinct photographs and 354 source clusters. These saved-prediction comparisons do not establish physical invariance, general cross-session transfer or an economic optimum.

The one-page portrait PDF summarizes improvements and the twelve professor-request groups. Current and historical comparisons are distinguished in `Comparison_Controls.csv`. The core swatch-ID task does not require semantic species/product labels; semantic-equivalence analysis would be a separate question.

The manuscript and Supplementary Material are maintained in the private submission package. Submission letters, author-confirmation material and private reviewer-access links are outside this public release. Repeated real-leather captures, capture records, field unknowns and exact historical random pools remain unresolved. No new scientific experiment was performed for this publication synchronization.

The editorial practice follows oral-paper-skill: identify the contribution delta, state what each comparison holds fixed and extract a bounded lesson. The official abstract of [Training on the Test Task Confounds Evaluation and Emergence](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/oral/31791) was inspected as an example of comparison framing. This is abstract-level editorial guidance, not evidence for the material-retrieval results or a full-paper rigor assessment.
